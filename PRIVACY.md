# Ticker privacy policy

Ticker collects nothing about you.

- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs that report usage.
- **Camera:** images are processed on your phone to read prices and are discarded immediately. They are never stored or sent anywhere. The gallery button uses Android's photo picker, so Ticker only sees the single photo you choose.
- **Network:** the app downloads public exchange rates from the European Central Bank via api.frankfurter.dev, and from the fawazahmed0 currency API via cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev. These requests contain no personal data; like any web request they reveal your IP address and the time of the request to those services, which handle it under their own privacy policies.
- **On-device storage:** your favourites, settings and the last downloaded rates are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Text selection:** when you choose "Convert" on selected text in another app, that text is read on the phone to find a price and is not stored or sent.
- **Permissions:** Camera (to read prices; optional) and Internet (to download rates).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/szlukabence/ticker/issues.
